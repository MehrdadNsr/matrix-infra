FAQ
What is the Claude Startups program and can I apply?
Claude Startups is Anthropic’s program for early-stage founders building on Claude. Members get access to offers, tools, and events to help run their business using Claude. Interest in the program has been far higher than we expected, and our current cohort is now full, so we’re not accepting new applications right now. Subscribe to the
Claude Startup newsletter
to hear when applications open for the next cohort and get the latest updates for startups building on Claude.
If I’m a member, do I still have access to the program?
Yes, the program is continuing for current members. Your membership includes offers on AI-native tools through the Claude Startup Stack, access to group sessions with Anthropic’s Applied AI team every other week, and invites to Claude Startup events, hackathons, and community meetups.
I applied and wasn’t accepted. What happens to my application?
Our current cohort is now full, so we weren’t able to move forward with applications that were still in review, and we’ve emailed everyone affected. While we didn’t have space for more members this cohort, we encourage you to apply when applications open for the next one.
What is the Claude Startup Stack?
Offers from AI-native companies building on Claude, across sales, design, data, meetings, and engineering. Members redeem each offer with a code in the Claude Console.
How do Applied AI sessions work?
Applied AI sessions are live group sessions for members, held every other week in a webinar format. Each one covers a topic and includes an AMA with Anthropic’s Applied AI team. Members get a link to register by email and in the Claude Startups portal.
Can I use credits when accessing Claude on AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure?
No. Claude Startups credits apply to the first-party Claude API through Claude Console. They can’t be used on AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, or other third-party platforms.
I’m backed by a VC in Anthropic’s partner network. Can I get API credits?
You may be eligible to receive up to $100K in additional API credits, claimed through your VC.
How can I keep up with what’s new for startups?
Subscribe to the

for founder stories, playbooks, and product news.
